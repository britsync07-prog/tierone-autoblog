# Access & Pricing — AI Tool Digest

## Public Access Policy
AI Tool Digest is an independent technical journalism platform delivering open-access engineering intelligence, model benchmarks, and architectural guides. All content is 100% free and publicly accessible with zero paywalls.

## Tiers & Limits

### Free Reader & Builder Access
- **Price**: $0 / month (USD, GBP, CAD, AUD)
- **Paywall**: None. Zero gating.
- **Features Included**:
  - Full-length, deep-dive architectural teardowns
  - Interactive comparison tables & empirical benchmarks
  - Step-by-step developer walkthroughs and prompt engineering guides
  - Daily breaking AI news dispatches
  - Machine-readable `/llms.txt`, `/llms-full.txt`, and `/mcp-actions.json`
- **Limits**: Unlimited page views, no registration or account required.

### Autonomous AI Agents & Crawlers
- **Price**: $0 / month
- **Access Level**: Full public read access across all articles, sitemaps, and feeds.
- **Allowed User-Agents**: `Googlebot`, `Bingbot`, `Applebot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Claude-SearchBot`, `Google-Extended`, and standards-compliant AI agents.
- **Guidelines**: Respect crawl rate headers; do not scrape for uncredited mass republishing without proper attribution.

## Commercial Transparency
- **Sponsored Content**: Any sponsored technical analysis is strictly demarcated with clear disclosure.
- **Affiliate Links**: If affiliate partner links are present, they are declared with `rel="sponsored nofollow"` per Google Search Guidelines.
- **Editorial Integrity**: Tools and models are evaluated solely on empirical performance, safety, and utility.
