## Phase Boundary Context
Phase 7 delivers daily tech and AI news ingestion and publishing.
Plan 07-03 delivers the AI & Tech News Silo (`/ai-news`) & Schema.org `NewsArticle` integration (NEWS-03):
1. **Silo Taxonomy Expansion**: Expands `SILO_SLUGS` in `lib/silos.ts` from 5 to 6, adding `'ai-news'` ("AI & Tech News").
2. **Reverse-Chronological News Hub (`/ai-news`)**: Renders dedicated news hub displaying breaking news posts sorted by publication date, with `CollectionPage` JSON-LD schema.
3. **Google News Schema (`NewsArticle`)**: Generates Google-compliant Schema.org `NewsArticle` structured data for any post under `ai-news`.
4. **Header Navigation Update**: Adds "AI News" link to `components/site-header.tsx` for immediate crawler and reader discovery.
5. **Initial Seed News Post & Shard**: Creates `content/posts/anthropic-claude-3-7-sonnet-hybrid-reasoning-release.mdx` and `content/keywords/ai-news/registry.json` to establish instant operational parity.
6. **Integrity Gates Alignment**: Updates `scripts/check-crawl.mjs` and `scripts/check-registry.mjs` so full CI suites pass with all 6 silos.

## Decisions Handled
- **D-01 (Canonical Silo Inclusion):** `ai-news` is a full first-class silo in `lib/silos.ts`, sharing the same flat URL hierarchy (`/blog/{slug}`) and silo landing (`/ai-news`).
- **D-02 (Schema Discrimination):** News posts emit `NewsArticle` schema to qualify for Google News, Top Stories, and Discover, while evergreen guides retain `BlogPosting`.
- **D-03 (Crawl & Registry Parity):** All 6 silos must have active posts and registry shards to pass `npm run check:crawl` and `npm run check:registry`.

<threat_model>
### Threat Ref T-07-05: Schema.org Validation Errors in Rich Results & Broken Silo Traversal
- **Threat:** Emitting invalid or malformed `NewsArticle` JSON-LD (missing `headline`, `datePublished`, or `publisher`) causes Google Rich Results test to flag errors, preventing appearance in Google News, Top Stories carousels, or Discover feeds. In addition, adding a 6th silo could cause crawl-gate failures or orphaned silo landings if not wired through static params and split sitemaps.
- **Mitigation:** Structured data uses proven `next-seo` v7 typed props with explicit `NewsArticle` type mapping. Canonical URLs, ISO dates, author details, and publisher logos are sourced from `lib/site.ts`. The landing page is registered in `SILO_SLUGS`, generating static pages at build time and placing `/ai-news` into sitemap shard 1 alongside the other 5 silo landings.
- **Verification:** `tests/news-silo.test.ts` checks that `buildArticleJsonLd` returns a valid `NewsArticle` schema payload with mandatory fields, and `scripts/check-crawl.mjs` verifies that `/ai-news` is indexed in sitemap shard 1 with 200 status.
</threat_model>
