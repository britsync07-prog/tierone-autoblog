<plan_context>
## Phase Boundary Context
Phase 7 delivers daily tech and AI news ingestion and publishing.
Plan 07-02 establishes the Journalistic News Article Synthesizer & Schema Compliance engine (NEWS-02):
1. **Journalistic Tech Synthesis**: Generates high-authority, analytical news coverage from ingested feed items using the free-tier LLM client (`lib/pipeline/llm.ts`).
2. **Strict Zod Contract**: Enforces H1 title (50–60 chars), meta description (150–160 chars), slug format, TL;DR ($\le 100$ words), 3–4 FAQs, and structured MDX body.
3. **Mandatory Section Headers**: Requires "## The Breakdown: What Happened", "## Why It Matters for Developers & Engineers", "## Industry Impact & Ecosystem Context", and "## Verified Sources & Primary Documentation".
4. **Anti-Buzzword & EEAT Quality Filters**: Rejects AI fluff, buzzwords, and unsubstantiated claims. Links back directly to verified source documentation.
5. **Fail-Closed Frontmatter Assembly**: Validates the serialized frontmatter directly through `draftSchema` (`lib/pipeline/draft-schema.ts`) to ensure zero Content Collections build failures.
6. **Offline Mock Resilience**: Supports deterministic offline mocking for CI/local test environments where LLM API keys are unset.

## Decisions Handled
- **D-01 (Strict Journalistic Structure):** News posts must provide genuine technical information gain, not shallow summaries. Four specific H2 sections are hard-required.
- **D-02 (Single-Source Frontmatter Schema):** All news frontmatter must pass `draftSchema.parse()` before being saved to `content/posts/`.
- **D-03 (Banned Phrases & Tone):** AI clichés ('game-changer', 'delve', 'revolutionize') trigger rejection and reprompting/sanitization.
</plan_context>

<threat_model>
### Threat Ref T-07-03: Hallucinatory Claims, Fabricated Citations & Low-Quality AI Prose
- **Threat:** LLM generates fictitious quotes, hallucinated technical specs, fabricated benchmark scores, or dead citation links, risking manual penalties under Google's helpful content and search spam policies.
- **Mitigation:** The synthesis prompt strictly constrains coverage to factual data supplied in the ingestion candidate payload, mandates direct outbound markdown linking to the primary reporting URL, requires 4 structured analytical sections, and runs an automated anti-cliché filter (`BANNED_EDITORIAL_PHRASES`) before output acceptance.
- **Verification:** `tests/news-synthesis.test.ts` asserts that synthesized articles retain source attribution links, contain no banned clichés, and adhere to strict editorial section contracts.

### Threat Ref T-07-04: Frontmatter Schema Validation Failure Breaking Next.js & Content Collections Build
- **Threat:** Generated frontmatter contains invalid characters, unescaped quotes, missing fields, or slug formatting errors, causing Content Collections compilation or `next build` to fail during automated deployment.
- **Mitigation:** `assembleNewsPostMdx` validates the structured object through `draftSchema.parse()` prior to serializing markdown. String fields are escaped against YAML delimiter collisions, and slugs are constrained to `^[a-z0-9]+(?:-[a-z0-9]+)*$`.
- **Verification:** Unit tests in `tests/news-synthesis.test.ts` feed generated frontmatter into `draftSchema.parse()` and verify that serialization is completely compatible with Content Collections.
</threat_model>
